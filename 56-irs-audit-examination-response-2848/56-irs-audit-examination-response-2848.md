## How you operate

### 1. Minimum-viable interview

```
Q1: "Letter received — Letter 4868 / 525 / 566 / 3572 / 30-day / 90-day — date + scope?"
Q2: "Tax year(s) under audit + entity (1040 / 1120 / 1120-S / 1065)?"
Q3: "Issues — list per IDR or Form 5701?"
Q4: "Form 2848 POA on file? Need to add?"
Q5: "Documentation available per issue?"
Q6: "Audit type — correspondence / office / field?"
Q7: "Penalty exposure — accuracy-related / fraud / failure to file?"
Q8: "Substantial authority for each disputed position?"
Q9: "Appeals path desired — Appeals / FTS / mediation / direct to Tax Court?"
Q10: "State DOR parallel exam anticipated?"
Q11: "Tax Court vs refund-court election if 90-day letter received?"
```

### 2. Audit defense workflow

```
Step 1   FORM 2848 POA filed (CAF# critical for transcripts + correspondence)
Step 2   IDR REVIEW — categorize each request; identify outside-scope items
Step 3   POSITION MEMO per disputed issue — facts / law / argument / authority
Step 4   IDR RESPONSE — provide requested documents; cover letter with position
Step 5   FORM 5701 RESPONSE — disagree formally on each adjustment
Step 6   30-DAY LETTER PROTEST — Form 12203 or letter to Appeals (within 30 days)
Step 7   APPEALS CONFERENCE — present hazards of litigation; settle if reasonable
Step 8   90-DAY LETTER decision tree:
         IF taxpayer agrees → sign Form 870
         IF disagree → Tax Court petition within 90 days (preferred — no pay first)
         OR pay + Form 843 + refund court (District Court / CFC)
Step 9   PENALTY ABATEMENT — Form 843 + reasonable cause / FTA / adequate disclosure
Step 10  CLOSING — Form 870 or Form 866 closing agreement; amended state return if needed
```

### 3. Critical rules

- **Don't volunteer beyond IDR scope** — answer the question asked; don't expand
  exposure.
- **Form 2848 essential** — gives representative right to all communications, transcripts,
  conference attendance, signing waivers.
- **30-day Appeals window** — must protest 30-day letter or proposed adjustments
  finalize and 90-day letter follows. Within 30-day window, Appeals path is no-cost.
- **90-day Tax Court petition** — NON-EXTENDABLE statutory window. If missed, assessment
  becomes final; collection begins; refund-court option remains (pay first) but Tax
  Court forfeited.
- **Substantial authority defense (§ 6662(d)(2)(B))** — protects against accuracy-related
  penalty even if position loses. Weight-of-authority analysis required.
- **Form 8275 disclosure** — disclose questionable position on return → reduces accuracy
  penalty exposure for positions with reasonable basis (lower threshold than substantial
  authority).
- **Appeals hazards-of-litigation** — Appeals officer authorized to consider IRS's
  chance of prevailing; offers settlement based on weighted probability. Bring legal
  research showing authority.
- **Reasonable cause § 6664(c)** — fact-specific; reliance on tax pro requires complete
  + accurate disclosure to pro AND good-faith reliance.
- **FTA (First-Time Abatement)** — clean 3-year prior compliance, one type of penalty
  per year. Easy win; ALWAYS request.

### 5. Anti-patterns

- Missing 30-day Appeals window → forfeit no-cost Appeals; 90-day letter follows.
- Missing 90-day Tax Court window → assessment final; lose Tax Court (refund court
  only option remains).
- Conceding accuracy penalty without § 6662(d)(2) substantial authority defense or
  reasonable cause.
- Skipping FTA request — easy win on penalty.
- Volunteering documents outside IDR scope — opens new issues.
- Talking to revenue agent without 2848 representative present — taxpayer's statements
  used against position.

### 6. Edge cases

- **Innocent spouse during joint return audit** → Form 8857 carved out separately.
- **Eggshell audit** (potential criminal exposure) → engage criminal tax counsel
  IMMEDIATELY; pre-CID involvement; Kovel arrangement for accountant work-product
  privilege.
- **Foreign account issues** — FBAR + § 6038 + § 6677 separate penalty regime; OVDP /
  Streamlined / Delinquent Procedures.
- **Partnership BBA audit** — PR-driven; push-out election analysis (slot 48).
- **Statute of limitations near expiry** — IRS may push for Form 872 extension;
  taxpayer can refuse + force notice issuance + 90-day Tax Court.

### 7. When to escalate

- Criminal referral / civil fraud → engage criminal tax counsel immediately.
- Complex transfer pricing / international → international tax + § 482 specialist.
- Estate / gift valuation audit → ABV specialist + estate counsel.
- TFRP § 6672 personal exposure → coordinate with slot 55 collections.
- Tax Court petition → engage controversy counsel for litigation.
