### 3. Critical rules

**ERC mill claims**: most aggressive 2020-2023 promoter claims used "any government order
counts" or "supply chain ripple" theories. IRS guidance (Notice 2021-20, 2021-23, 2021-49,
2023-67) requires FULL or PARTIAL suspension of operations due to a governmental order
that has more than a NOMINAL effect (>10% of business operations). Mere precaution,
recommendation, or supplier issue does not qualify. If a claim was filed on shaky grounds:

- Voluntary Disclosure Program (extended — confirm current deadline) — return 80% of
  refund, keep 20%, avoid penalty + criminal exposure.
- Withdrawal Program for claims not yet paid — withdraw entirely.
- If audited: § 6662 20% accuracy-related penalty likely; § 6663 75% civil fraud
  possible; criminal § 7206 possible for fraudulent claims.

**R&D § 41**: legitimate claim requires contemporaneous documentation. Build process-of-
experimentation log (hypotheses, alternatives, tests, outcomes). Wage allocation must
match QRA hours. ASC method (14%) is more common than RRC for SMB. § 280C(c) reduces
deduction by credit OR elect reduced credit (13.5% net for C-Corp). Payroll-tax election
under § 41(h) for QSB (≤$5M GR, ≤5 years) lets credit offset FICA up to $500K/year
(post-IRA expansion).

**§ 174 (POST-2022)**: domestic R&D expenses MUST capitalize and amortize over 5 years
(SL half-year first); foreign R&D 15 years. Massive cash-flow drag for R&D-heavy companies
through 2026 unless Congress restores immediate expensing. § 41 credit still available
on the capitalized expenses — but deduction timing shifts. Form 3115 method-change
considerations for transition.

**Refund SoL § 6511(a)**: 3 years from return filed (or due date, later) OR 2 years from
payment (later). Refund claim before SoL closes. § 6511(d) special rules for NOL CB,
worthless securities, bad debts have longer windows.

**§ 6501 assessment SoL**: 3 years (6 if > 25% omission of gross income; unlimited if
fraud / no return). ERC audit risk window extends to 5 years for 2020 (CARES extension
§ 3134(l) added by ARPA — confirm) and arguably 6 if substantial omission. § 6511
refund SoL and § 6501 assessment SoL are independent.

**IRA energy credits**: prevailing wage + apprenticeship bonus for many credits (PTC/ITC
multiplier 5×) — must comply OR forfeit bonus. Domestic content adder, energy community
adder available. Recapture rules under § 50 for ITC dispositions in early years.

### 5. Anti-patterns

- Filing ERC on "revenue dropped > 20%" without partial-suspension or order-driven analysis.
- R&D claim with retrofitted wage allocations (no time logs).
- Skipping § 174 capitalization for post-2022 R&D — current-year deduction overstated.
- WOTC missed because 28-day Form 8850 window blew.
- IRA energy credit claimed without prevailing-wage compliance — bonus forfeited.
- Promoter contingent fee on prep work — Circular 230 § 10.27 violation.
- Filing 1139 / 1045 after 12-month deadline → forfeited; must file Form 1120-X / 1040-X
  + standard refund claim (longer wait).

### 6. Edge cases

- **ERC + PPP loan forgiveness coordination**: same wages can't double-dip; FAQ track.
- **R&D + § 41 + § 174 interaction with NOL** — capitalized § 174 increases NOL via
  reduced current expense, then 5-year amortization unwinds.
- **WOTC + state job credits** stacked — typically permitted, but state-specific.
- **Refund for closed years via § 6511(d)** — bad debts 7 yr, NOL CB special, worthless
  securities 7 yr, foreign tax 10 yr.
- **ERC promoter contingent fee** = ethical issue + likely Circular 230 violation.
